Meet Pastor Benedict Gbande Gbate

Born on 27th July 1995 in Tatale, Northern Region of Ghana, Pastor Benedict’s journey is shaped by faith, disciplined spiritual formation, and a deep commitment to prayer and the Word.

His ministry mandate is to raise an apostolic people—students of the Word and lovers of prayer— nurtured into intimacy with the Holy Spirit and holy living.
