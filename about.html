About Pastor Benedict Gbande Gbate

Servant of God, Leader, Teacher, and Friend

Biography

"To know Christ and make Him known"

Pastor Benedict Gbande Gbate was born on 27th July 1995 at Tatale in the Northern Region of Ghana. Born to Mr. Mahama Emmanuel Kwasi Gbate and Mrs. Lydia Gbate. He comes from a family of seven children, comprising six brothers and one sister, and he is the third born.

Faith Journey and Christian Foundation

Benedict’s faith journey is deeply rooted in a powerful family testimony. Around 2001, his father suffered a severe illness that persisted for about a year. Despite numerous hospital visits across the country, medical interventions proved helpless. Through divine intervention, God used a minister from the Assemblies of God Ghana, Victory Assembly, Tatale, and his father received miraculous healing. At the time, Benedict’s parents were already Christians of the Roman Catholic tradition, with a strong Charismatic orientation, devoted to prayer, night vigils, and spiritual disciplines. However, this miraculous encounter marked a decisive turning point. In 2002, the family joined the Assemblies of God Church, where his parents deepened their walk with Christ, and Benedict and his siblings were intentionally led into a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. Growing up under deeply devoted, prayerful, and disciplined parents, Benedict was nurtured in the fear of God, obedience, holiness, and service. His childhood and adolescent years were characterized by active involvement in church life and spiritual formation.

Call to Ministry and Early Service

Pastor Benedict sensed a clear call of God upon his life at a very young age, around Primary Five. He openly shared this conviction with his parents and began consciously preparing himself for ministry. He actively served in the children’s department, Sunday school, children’s choir, Bible drills, coaching, and various church programs, eagerly learning and growing in grace. Throughout Junior High School and Senior High School, his commitment to ministry deepened as he actively participated in campus ministries, conferences, evangelism, and discipleship programs. He also served in his local church by teaching adult Sunday school classes and leading Bible study sessions, demonstrating an early grace for teaching and leadership.

Ministry Journey

. Benedict had a strong desire to enroll in Bible school as early as 2015. However, despite several attempts, he consistently sensed a divine restraint, discerning that the time appointed by God had not yet come. In 2022, he made another serious attempt, even engaging his head pastor, church deacons, and seeking parental approval. While on his way to do so, he received a clear spiritual ministration that it was still not time. In obedience, he surrendered the desire and waited upon the Lord. During this waiting season, inspired by Psalm 85:6, he led intense prayer engagements and established a Prayer Movement, organizing prayer meetings, revival programs, and sustained spiritual exercises. In God’s appointed time, he received divine approval and successfully gained admission to Bible School in November 2023 at The Assemblies of God Theological Seminary, North Campus Kumbungu Tamale. During his training, he served diligently and was privileged to serve as Chairman of the Preaching Conference Committee, where sermons were vetted to meet homiletical standards for preaching. He later also served as Vice Chairman of the Editorial Board. He successfully graduated in November 2025 with a Diploma in Theology, marking a major milestone in his ministerial journey. .

Current Ministry Expression

n 2025, the Prayer Movement transitioned into an online ministry expression known as The Qodesh Mountains—a platform committed to prayer, discipleship, spiritual revival, and raising believers grounded in holiness and purpose. Throughout 2025, the ministry began weekly teaching sessions, which continue consistently to date. .

University Ministry and Leadership

While at the university, Benedict continued to respond faithfully to God’s call. He devoted himself to teaching, discipling, and mentoring students through fellowships and prayer initiatives, both directly and indirectly. By the mercies of God, he served as President of the Assemblies of God Campus Ministry from 2019 to 2021. In his final year, he also served in a significant leadership role as Chairman of the Students’ Christian Council, where he coordinated over eighteen central campus denominations and thirty-two non-denominational Christian groups. His leadership tenure concluded successfully in 2021, the same year he completed his undergraduate studies. After graduation, he undertook his mandatory national service, during which he continued to testify of Christ and serve faithfully in ministry.
