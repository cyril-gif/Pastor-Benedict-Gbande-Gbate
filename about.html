Biography

Pastor Benedict Gbande Gbate

“My greatest joy is seeing people transformed by the power of God’s Word and walking in the purpose He has for their lives.”

About Pastor Benedict

Born on 27th July 1995 in Tatale, Northern Region of Ghana. Pastor Benedict is devoted to purity, holiness, prayer and Word-centered discipleship.

Diploma in Theology (Nov 2025) The Qodesh Mountains Apostolic Mandate

Early Life

Benedict Gbande Gbate was born on 27th July 1995 at Tatale in the Northern Region of Ghana. Born to Mr. Mahama Emmanuel Kwasi Gbate and Mrs. Lydia Gbate, he comes from a family of seven children—six brothers and one sister—and he is the third born.

Educational Background

Benedict began his formal education at the Roman Catholic Primary and Junior High School, Tatale, and proceeded to the Northern School of Business, Tatale (2012–2015).

In 2016, he gained admission to UDS, Wa Campus, studying Diploma in Integrated Community Development and completed in 2018. He pursued B.A. in Environment and Resource Management (2018–2021) and completed in 2021.

Faith Journey and Christian Foundation

Around 2001, his father suffered a severe illness for about a year. After many hospital visits proved helpless, God used a minister from Assemblies of God Ghana, Victory Assembly, Tatale, and his father received miraculous healing.

Though his parents were Roman Catholic Christians with charismatic devotion, this miracle marked a turning point. In 2002, the family joined the Assemblies of God Church and were intentionally nurtured into a deeper walk with Christ.

Call to Ministry and Early Service

Benedict sensed a clear call of God around Primary Five. He served in children’s ministry, Sunday school, choir, Bible drills, coaching and church programs. Throughout JHS and SHS, his commitment deepened through evangelism, conferences and discipleship.

University Ministry and Leadership

By God’s mercies, he served as President of the Assemblies of God Campus Ministry (2019–2021). In his final year, he became Chairman of the Students’ Christian Council coordinating over 18 campus denominations and 32 non-denominational Christian groups.

Bible School Training and Ministry Development

Benedict desired Bible school from 2015 but sensed divine restraint. In 2022, he again attempted and received spiritual ministration to wait. During this season (Psalm 85:6), he led intense prayer engagements and established a Prayer Movement.

He gained admission in Nov 2023 to the Assemblies of God Theological Seminary, North Campus (Kumbungu, Tamale). He served as Chairman of the Preaching Conference Committee and later Vice Chairman of the Editorial Board. He graduated in Nov 2025 with a Diploma in Theology.

Current Ministry Expression

In 2025, the Prayer Movement transitioned into an online ministry expression called The Qodesh Mountains— committed to prayer, discipleship, spiritual revival and raising believers grounded in holiness and purpose. Weekly teaching sessions began in 2025 and continue to date.

Core Values, Mandate, and Conviction

Pastor Benedict is committed to purity, holiness, godly character, and training believers in the ways of Jesus Christ. His mandate is to raise an apostolic people—students of the Word and lovers of prayer—growing in intimacy with the Holy Spirit and integrity of life.

“My greatest joy is seeing people transformed by the power of God’s Word and walking in the purpose He has for their lives.”
