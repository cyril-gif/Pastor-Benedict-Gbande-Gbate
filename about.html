Biography of Pastor Benedict Gbande Gbate

A journey of faith, prayer, holiness, leadership, and ministry development.

Early Life

Benedict Gbande Gbate was born on 27th July 1995 at Tatale in the Northern Region of Ghana. He was born to Mr. Mahama Emmanuel Kwasi Gbate and Mrs. Lydia Gbate. He comes from a family of seven children—six brothers and one sister—and he is the third born.

Educational Background

Benedict began his formal education at the Roman Catholic Primary and Junior High School, Tatale. He proceeded to the Northern School of Business, Tatale for secondary education (2012–2015).

In 2016, he gained admission to the University for Development Studies (UDS), Wa Campus and studied Diploma in Integrated Community Development (2016–2018). He continued at the same institution to pursue a Bachelor of Arts in Environment and Resource Management (2018–2021), completing in 2021.

Faith Journey & Christian Foundation

Around 2001, his father suffered a severe illness for about a year. After many hospital visits with no solution, God used a minister from Assemblies of God Ghana, Victory Assembly, Tatale, and his father received miraculous healing.

Though his parents were Roman Catholic Christians with strong charismatic orientation, this miracle became a turning point. In 2002, the family joined the Assemblies of God Church, deepening their walk with Christ and leading Benedict and his siblings into a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

Call to Ministry & Early Service

Benedict sensed a clear call of God around Primary Five. He served actively in children’s ministry, Sunday school, choir, Bible drills, coaching, and church programs—learning and growing in grace.

Through JHS and SHS, his commitment deepened through campus ministries, conferences, evangelism, and discipleship. He taught adult Sunday school and led Bible study sessions, showing early grace for teaching and leadership.

University Ministry & Leadership

At the university, he continued teaching, mentoring, and discipling students through fellowships and prayer initiatives. He served as President of the Assemblies of God Campus Ministry (2019–2021).

In his final year, he served as Chairman of the Students’ Christian Council, coordinating over 18 campus denominations and 32 non-denominational Christian groups. His tenure concluded successfully in 2021, the same year he completed his undergraduate studies.

Bible School Training & Ministry Development

Benedict desired Bible school as early as 2015 but sensed divine restraint until God’s appointed time. In 2022, he attempted again but received spiritual ministration that it was still not time. In obedience, he waited on the Lord.

Inspired by Psalm 85:6, he led intense prayer engagements and established a Prayer Movement, organizing prayer meetings, revival programs, and sustained spiritual exercises.

In God’s time, he gained admission in November 2023 to the Assemblies of God Theological Seminary (North Campus, Kumbungu–Tamale). He served as Chairman of the Preaching Conference Committee and later Vice Chairman of the Editorial Board. He graduated in November 2025 with a Diploma in Theology.

Current Ministry Expression

In 2025, the Prayer Movement transitioned into an online ministry expression known as The Qodesh Mountains— committed to prayer, discipleship, spiritual revival, and raising believers grounded in holiness and purpose. Weekly teaching sessions began in 2025 and continue consistently to date.

Core Values, Mandate & Conviction

Pastor Benedict is deeply committed to purity, holiness, and godly character, believing every believer is called to live holy as God is holy.

His mandate is to raise an apostolic people—students of the Word and lovers of prayer—into intimacy with the Holy Spirit, nurturing quality relationship with the Father and the Son, Jesus Christ. He carries a charge to train and equip believers in uncompromising faith, holiness, integrity, and strong character.

“My greatest joy is seeing people transformed by the power of God’s Word and walking in the purpose He has for their lives.”
